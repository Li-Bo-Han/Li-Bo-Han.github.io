Computer Science and Technology
(Joint Training Program with Accounting Major, University of International Business and Economics)

A cross-university program by Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT) (Computer Science) and University of International Business and Economics (UIBE) (Accounting). Students are full BUPT undergraduates — the degree is a B.E. in Computer Science from BUPT.

FAQs

✅ I am a full BUPT student, majoring in Computer Science.
✅ My CS training is identical to BUPT’s standard CS core curriculum.
✅ The accounting component comes from UIBE’s Accounting major, integrated as part of a new joint program (first cohort, 2024–2028).

This is a new joint bachelor's program — first cohort (2024–2028). It integrates BUPT's Computer Science core curriculum with UIBE's Accounting major courses. My CS training follows BUPT's standard CS track; the accounting courses are formally added from UIBE, not a minor or a second degree.

Computer Science and Technology is a key major of BUPT and a national first-class undergraduate program construction project (2019). Accounting at UIBE's International Business School has been offered since 1985 and was also selected as a national first-class undergraduate program in 2019.

Degrees

A 4-year program awarding a Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) degree, conferred by BUPT. The degree certificate includes signatures from the presidents of both BUPT and UIBE, recognizing the joint nature of the program.

CS and accounting courses are integrated throughout the four years — there is no separate "major selection" stage within this program.
